## How to Read This Document

Each entry captures a moment where the heading shifted — or deepened, or clarified. Over time, patterns emerge:

- **Recurring themes** reveal what you're actually about (even when the surface work changes)
- **Catalysts** show you what sparks your excitement (people, ideas, collisions between worlds)
- **Connections** reveal the mycelium — how seemingly separate projects are actually one root system
- **Compost** shows what you had to let go of or transform to move forward

This is the fractal at the life level. The small movements of daily excitement set the pattern for the whole.
